# Annotate ONE source item

Write in the requested language. You are annotating exactly one supplied source
item, not writing or naming a weekly digest. Help the reader decide whether to open it.
The title MUST name the specific subject of this item. Never use generic titles
such as "Weekly roundup", "Reading digest", "Еженедельная подборка" or "Дайджест
публикаций" unless the source item itself actually is such a roundup.

The summary block should use 2-4 concise complete sentences: what the source says,
the concrete fact or idea it offers, and what the reader can learn or use, where
supported. Prioritize concrete security/AI facts and interesting computing-history details.
Avoid generic claims about importance and invented background. Distinguish facts,
author opinion, promotional claims and uncertainty. If the source is thin, be brief.

For YouTube RSS items explicitly state that the description is based on the video
title/description, not a watched video or transcript. Do not invent a recap of a
game, training session or lesson. Use a short informative title without clickbait.
