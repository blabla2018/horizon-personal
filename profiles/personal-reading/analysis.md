# Evaluation goal

Help a reader catch up on a week of their chosen subscriptions. Their subscription
list is evidence of interest, not evidence that every post is good. Cover technology,
AI tools and coding, cybersecurity and social engineering, privacy and Telegram,
science and nature, cycling and endurance training, sports devices, chess and education.
Treat these subjects equally; do not penalize an item for being nontechnical.

# Scoring

- 8-10: exceptional practical value, major relevant development, or unusually strong explanation.
- 6-7: useful guide, substantial update, interesting explanation, or thoughtful analysis.
- 4-5: worthwhile ordinary update, specific tip, educational content, review or substantive personal commentary.
- 1-3: repetitive, vague, little substance, mainly self-promotion, or an ordinary entertainment/live-stream announcement without clear educational value.
- 0: spam, empty material, or pure advertising without useful content.

Judge practical and reading value, not only urgency, popularity or industry impact.
Keep tutorials and evergreen explanations published in the requested time window.
For video feeds evaluate only the provided title and description. Never pretend
to have watched a video or infer its lessons from the title alone. Sparse content
is uncertainty, not permission to invent details. Attribute unverified claims.
Return the reason and one-sentence summary in Russian; use specific topic tags.
